### Mode 1: 🎮 Default Air Mouse & Media Remote

  - **2-Finger Scroll**: Vertical scrolling through webpages and documents.

## ⚡ Setup & Pairing Guide

### Step 1: Install the APK
1. Download **[AirConnectPro-v0.5.apk](https://github.com/KoushikDesu/Air-mouse-controller/releases/download/v0.5/AirConnectPro-v0.5.apk)**.
2. Tap the file to install on Android.

### Step 2: Grant Permissions
1. Open **Air Connect Pro**.
2. Grant Nearby Devices / Bluetooth permissions when prompted.

### Step 3: Pair with Laptop
#### Method A: Microsoft Swift Pair (Windows 10/11)
1. Turn on Bluetooth on your laptop (**Settings → Bluetooth & devices**).
2. In the phone app, tap **"📡 PAIR / SWIFT PAIR"**.
3. A Windows notification toast appears in the bottom-right corner of your laptop screen:
   > **Air Mouse Pro**  
   > *New Bluetooth mouse found. Connect to this device?*
4. Click **Connect** and pairing is complete!

#### Method B: Manual Bluetooth Pairing
1. On your laptop: **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth**.
2. Select **Air Mouse Pro**.
3. The app status turns 🟢 **Connected • Ready**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q: How do I drag and drop files or select text?
> **Answer**: Simply touch and hold your finger on the trackpad for 300ms without lifting. You will feel a firm haptic click and see "HOLD TO DRAG ACTIVE". Now drag your finger across the screen to select or move files, and lift to release!

#### Q: How do I change the Fullscreen Trackpad wallpaper?
> **Answer**: Tap the 3-bar menu (or sidebar arrow) ➜ **Settings** ➜ tap **"Pick from Gallery"** to select any photo, and adjust the **Dimming Slider** with the live preview box!

#### Q: How do I adjust volume while using Fullscreen Trackpad?
> **Answer**: Drag your thumb up or down along the right edge of the screen.
